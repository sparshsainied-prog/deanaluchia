Is There Anybody Out There?

Uplit, with aliens. Deana's new novel is about why we should all talk to strangers: how tricky it can be to make friends as an adult, and why it's worth going on looking until you find your people.

Out now as an ebook. Paperback to follow.

There's always a dog

Every story Deana writes has at least one dog in it: Great Danes, Chihuahuas, Border Terriers and mixed breeds, cavorting across the chapters and bringing comfort and laughter to characters struggling to make sense of the world.
